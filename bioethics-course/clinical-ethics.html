Ethics in Decision Making for Healthcare Professionals

2 Series Program 6 Days Each Series Certificate Available

Ethics in Decision Making for Healthcare Professionals is an intensive bioethics training program developed by CBMH FK-KMK UGM to strengthen ethical reasoning and decision-making skills among healthcare professionals.

This program equips participants with practical ethical frameworks, communication skills, legal understanding, and critical analysis to address complex dilemmas in clinical practice, healthcare systems, and emerging health technologies.

Available for Institutional Collaboration
Training available for hospitals, universities, and healthcare institutions.

Healthcare professionals increasingly face complex ethical dilemmas involving patient autonomy, cultural values, legal considerations, emerging technologies, and institutional policies.

This training program provides participants with practical tools and ethical frameworks to analyze, communicate, and make responsible healthcare decisions within the Indonesian socio-cultural and regulatory context.

Learning activities include interactive lectures, case-based discussions, role-playing, simulations, debates, workshops, and reflective learning facilitated by experts in bioethics, clinical ethics, law, and healthcare practice.

  • Medical Confidentiality in Digital Era

  • Ethics of IVF & Embryo Moral Status
  • Prenatal Diagnosis & Therapy Ethics
  • Ethical Challenges in Neonatal & Pediatric Care
  • Ethics in Elderly Patient Care

Series 2: Systemic Ethics, Legal Issues, and Contemporary Challenges

Participants explore ethical-legal dilemmas at institutional and systemic levels, including emerging issues related to technology, healthcare policy, and resource allocation.

  • Personalized Medicine Ethics

  • Legal Protection for Healthcare Professionals
  • Medical Dispute Resolution

  • Justice & Resource Allocation Ethics
